# AI Background Remover

## Tagline
Free AI background remover that runs in your browser. No signup. No watermark.

## Description
Tired of background removers that watermark everything or charge after 3 uses? We built a free tool that:

- Runs 100% in your browser (your photos never leave your device)
- Uses open-source AI models
- No signup required
- No watermark
- Works on mobile and desktop
- Free forever

Powered by briaai/RMBG-1.4 model via Transformers.js.
